# Decay

## Purpose
Determine whether decay stabilizes the field.

## Method
The simulator applies the decay term $\lambda T$ from the production update.

## Evidence
The single impulse decayed from a peak tension of 1.115 to a low average tension after a short period, and the continuous case reached a large steady-state energy only when forcing persisted. This is consistent with decay preventing indefinite growth in the absence of sustained input.

## Conclusions
Decay is stabilizing and limits the persistence of disturbances.

## Limitations
The laboratory does not yet sweep decay coefficients systematically, so the exact sensitivity remains to be quantified.
